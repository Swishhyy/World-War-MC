# Education proposal

[All guides](README.md) · [Settlement production](settlement-production.md)

Education should add a useful settlement service without preventing a new town from supporting itself. These are proposed rules; this update does not add education restrictions.

| Stage | Learning | Jobs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | Every adult starts here | Farming, forestry, gathering, mining, fishing, hauling and basic guard duty |
| Literate | An adult studies with a researcher at a lectern with books nearby; lessons cost paper and loaded teaching time | Research assistance and ordinary workshop production |
| Apprentice | A literate adult trains beside an experienced worker using the profession's real supplies | Advanced metallurgy, enchanting and later machines |

Children keep their current childhood: play, meals, housing and safety, with no job assignment. Schooling begins in adulthood. A researcher alternates scroll production and teaching rather than doing both simultaneously. The banner should show the student's progress and the precise pause reason.

Keep one starter researcher available without an education gate so the town can bootstrap literacy. Basic bronze forging and repairs must also remain available. Require apprenticeships only for advanced work such as steel forging, enchanting or future engineering, after the necessary settlement research. Education belongs to each citizen, survives job changes and saving, and never comes from idle time in unloaded chunks.

Existing working citizens should retain their current qualifications. Skilled workers can mentor newcomers so losing one teacher does not permanently block progression. Happiness can modestly improve lesson speed, without making unhappy villagers unable to learn.
